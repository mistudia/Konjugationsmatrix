# Signalwörter und Usage – aktueller Stand

Dropdown: genau 3 Signalwörter pro Zeit. „Auch eintippbar“ zählt als richtig, steht aber nicht im Dropdown. Usage: der ganze Eintrag, jeder Teil zwischen „•“ und jedes Wort in einer Klammer ist richtig.

## Englisch
| Zeit | Signalwörter (Dropdown) | Auch eintippbar | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Simple Present | always · every day · never | sometimes, usually, often | routines • focus on repetition |
| Present Progressive/Continuous | now · at the moment · right now | currently | action happening now • focus on the moment now and/or duration |
| Simple Past | yesterday · last week · ago | last year, in 2010 | standard past tense • focus on the fact that the event happened at all |
| Past Progressive/Continuous | while · at that moment · at 8 o'clock yesterday | all day yesterday, this time last year | action in progress in the past • focus on the moment then and/or duration |
| Present Perfect | already · just · yet | so far, ever, recently | completed past action with present result • focus on the fact that the event has an importance now |
| Present Perfect Progressive/Continuous | since · for · lately | all day, how long | past action continuing until now • focus on duration up to now |
| Past Perfect | before · after · by the time | until then | action before another past action • focus on the fact that the earlier event happened at all |
| Past Perfect Progressive/Continuous | all day before · for hours before · for a long time before | for ten minutes before | action before another past action • focus on duration before that moment |
| Will Future | probably · I think · perhaps | tomorrow I think, next week probably, maybe, I'm sure, tomorrow, next week | future action (predictions, spontaneous decisions) • focus on opinion or sudden decision |
| Future Progressive/Continuous | at this time tomorrow · tomorrow at 8 · tomorrow afternoon | – | action in progress in the future • focus on the moment then and/or duration |
| Future Perfect | by then · by Friday · by tomorrow | – | future action before another future action • focus on the result by then |
| Future Perfect Progressive/Continuous | for two hours by then · for ten years by then · for a week by then | – | future action before another future action • focus on duration by then |
| Going to Future | tomorrow as planned · next week as planned · look at those clouds | tonight, next month, tomorrow, next week | future action (plans, intentions) • focus on the plan or visible evidence |
